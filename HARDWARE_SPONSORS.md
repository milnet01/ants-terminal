## How to be listed

Subscribe to the 🚀 Hardware milestone backer tier (one-time, $250+)
on the [Sponsors profile](https://github.com/sponsors/milnet01),
then reply to the welcome email with:

1. Your preferred display name (or "anonymous").
2. (Optional) A one-line bio + link — company, personal site,
   social handle, or relevant project.
3. (Optional) Whether you'd like the optional 30-minute video call
   to discuss roadmap priorities.

Listings appear within 48 hours of the email reply. Sponsors who
fund a *specific* goal (rather than the open pool) get named in the
CHANGELOG of the release where that goal lands, plus a permanent
entry in the "Funded milestones" section above.

## Acknowledgement durability

Funded-milestone listings are permanent — they're a historical
record of who got each goal across the line. Cancellations don't
apply (these are one-off contributions, not recurring
subscriptions). Reply if you'd like your listing removed for any
reason; it's your name and your call.
