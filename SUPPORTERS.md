## How to be listed

Subscribe at any tier on the [Sponsors profile](https://github.com/sponsors/milnet01),
then reply to the welcome email with:

1. Your preferred display name (GitHub username, real name, company
   name, or "anonymous" — all welcome).
2. Which project(s) you'd like to be acknowledged in — Ants Terminal,
   Vestige, or both. Defaults to both if you don't specify.
3. (Optional) A one-line bio + link (your site, company page, social
   handle) — shown alongside your name on the higher tiers.

I update this file with the next release after a sponsor opts in;
typical lag is 1–2 weeks.

---

## Removed sponsors

When a sponsor cancels or pauses their subscription, their listing
is retained for the duration of the release that names them in the
CHANGELOG (so the historical acknowledgement stays accurate), and
then quietly removed from this file. Reply to the cancellation
notice if you'd like immediate removal instead.
